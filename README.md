## 01 / A little about my work

I'm a data professional focused on using analytical thinking, statistics, and machine learning to investigate practical business problems.

I care about the full journey: defining the question, checking the data, understanding what may be driving the outcome, choosing an appropriate method, and communicating what the evidence does and does not support.

My interests include customer behavior, business performance, experimentation, predictive modeling, and reliable data workflows.

> **My principle:** start with the decision, not the algorithm.
